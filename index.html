Who we are

A young Sephardi minyan in the heart of Encino.

Ateret Marjan is the youth congregation of Haichal Moshe, led by Rabbi Loloyan. We daven every day, learn together during the week, and come together for Shabbat, shiurim and community events. We follow the rulings of Maran Rabbi Ovadia Yosef.

  • Every dayShacharit at 6:30 AM, Mincha and Arvit each evening
  • ShabbatTwo Shacharit minyanim, shiurim and Seudat Shelishit
  • SundayShacharit at 8:45 AM, then breakfast together

Come for Shacharit. Stay for breakfast.

Every Sunday at 8:45 AM. Bring a friend.
